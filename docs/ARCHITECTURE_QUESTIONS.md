# RF Sentinel — Architecture Questions

These are unresolved questions, not hidden decisions. “Experiment” links refer to [EXPERIMENTS.md](research/EXPERIMENTS.md).

| ID | Question | Why it matters | Options | Evidence needed | Experiment if applicable | Decision blocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AQ-01 | Can supervised `rtl_power` satisfy the required RTL-SDR scan profiles, provenance, cadence, and recovery behavior? | Determines whether the first RTL adapter can remain a wrapper rather than need a direct API path. | Wrap `rtl_power`; use `librtlsdr`; use SoapySDR; custom pipeline. | Representative output/quality semantics, load, coverage, failure/recovery behavior on CM4. | Sustainable acquisition; FFT throughput; scan revisit; RTL-SDR stability; reboot/recovery. | Acceptance of the RTL command-line baseline (ADR-003). |
| AQ-02 | Can supervised `hackrf_sweep` satisfy equivalent HackRF profiles without unacceptable geometry/retune limitations? | Determines initial HackRF adapter suitability and measurement-quality rules. | Wrap `hackrf_sweep`; `libhackrf`; SoapySDR; custom pipeline. | Controlled sweep correctness, output semantics, resource use, device/recovery behavior. | Sustainable acquisition; FFT throughput; scan revisit; HackRF stability; reboot/recovery. | Acceptance of the HackRF command-line baseline (ADR-003). |
| AQ-03 | Does SoapySDR with target RTL-SDR/HackRF modules provide compatible discovery, capabilities, settings, stream stability, and reconnect behavior? | A generic API could reduce future device-specific code but may introduce module/ABI risk. | Evidence-gated SoapySDR adapter; direct device APIs; keep tool wrappers. | CM4 package/module matrix, capability mapping, acquisition and hot-plug tests. | Sustainable workload; USB stability; RTL/HackRF stability; reboot/recovery. | SoapySDR adapter adoption (ADR-008). |
| AQ-04 | What stable Linux identity and permission model will distinguish physical SDRs across reboots and reconnects? | Device reservation and profile assignment cannot safely rely on transient paths or names. | Hardware serial where present; udev mapping; composite identity; operator binding. | Actual devices’ identifiers, collision/replacement behavior, udev behavior, least-privilege setup. | USB stability and reboot/recovery experiments. | Production Device Manager identity rules. |
| AQ-05 | Is simultaneous multi-SDR operation required, and which CM4 USB/power topology sustains it? | Changes assignment, investigation capability, resource limits, and operational hardware. | Single SDR; sequential shared devices; concurrent distinct SDRs; dedicated investigation SDR. | Workload, USB contention, power, thermal, storage, and recovery evidence. | USB stability/multiple-SDR; thermal; sustainable workload. | Enabling multi-device concurrency and survey/investigation policy. |
| AQ-06 | Which baseline/noise-floor method and activity rules work across intended bands and environments? | Determines false-event/missed-event behavior, occupancy definition, and event evidence. | Fixed threshold; rolling percentile/robust baseline; spectral-change rule; combination. | Controlled present/absent/change datasets, sensitivity and limitations across profiles/devices. | Noise-floor estimation and activity detection. | Detector acceptance criteria and default profile policy. |
| AQ-07 | What frequency geometry, resolution, gain, calibration/reference, and quality flags can be compared across tools/devices? | PSD values must not be misrepresented as calibrated or comparable when they are not. | Device-specific semantics; normalized common subset; calibration workflow later. | Tool/API documentation plus controlled measurements and reproducibility. | FFT/measurement throughput; noise-floor/detection; relevant device stability experiments. | Final normalized measurement-field semantics and cross-device analytics policy. |
| AQ-08 | Which persistence representation and retention design meets CM4 write, capacity, query, and recovery needs? | Prevents premature database choice and unbounded storage while enabling reproducible reports. | Embedded structured store + partitioned measurement files; one store; other evidence-backed design. | Growth rates, write latency, retention cleanup correctness, query/report load, recovery behavior. | Disk throughput/capacity/endurance; historical processing/report generation; reboot/recovery. | Final storage architecture and retention thresholds (ADR-009). |
| AQ-09 | What storage reserve, queue capacity, and source-data behavior are safe under pressure? | Defines when to buffer, degrade derived work, pause scanning, and notify. | Policy-defined thresholds after measurement; different storage media; no IQ under pressure. | Capacity-growth and cleanup results; corruption/availability behavior. | Disk throughput/capacity/endurance; optional IQ impact. | Backpressure and storage-pressure configuration. |
| AQ-10 | What retry/backoff, restart, and operator-escalation policy gives reliable unattended behavior without retry storms? | Needed for tool failure, disconnect, network outage, and service crash. | Per-failure policies; common bounded policy; manual intervention after episode threshold. | Failure-injection outcomes, device/tool recovery rates, operational tolerances. | Reboot and automatic-recovery; RTL/HackRF stability. | Binding recovery settings and health-alert thresholds. |
| AQ-11 | Is one station service with child acquisition processes sufficiently isolated, or should acquisition/analytics later become separate services? | Affects deployment complexity, fault isolation, data consistency, and observability on CM4. | Initial single service; two services; more services. | Endurance/fault data showing impact of component failure/resource contention. | Sustainable workload; thermal; reboot/recovery; historical processing/report generation. | Revisiting ADR-006. |
| AQ-12 | What timezone, DST, time-synchronization, and clock-health rules govern schedules, timestamps, daily reports, and event ordering? | Required to make coverage/report periods and cross-restart history trustworthy. | UTC-only internal with configured presentation zone; station local time; defined DST transitions. | Deployment locale, time-source availability, operator/report expectations, clock-failure tests. | Reboot/recovery with time/network conditions; additional clock experiment if needed. | Schedule and daily-report time semantics. |
| AQ-13 | Which Telegram commands are read-only, which may change state, and what identity/authorization/audit model is acceptable? | Prevents Telegram from becoming an unauthorized control channel while satisfying control/report requirements. | Status/report only; restricted administrative commands; separate admin path. | Operator roles, Telegram identity constraints, threat model, audit/secret policy. | Transport failure/authorization tests; no SDR experiment required. | Telegram command set and security design. |
| AQ-14 | Under what policy may an investigation interrupt survey or use the same SDR? | Impacts coverage guarantees and event-response usefulness. | Never preempt; only distinct SDR; explicitly configured preemption; queued investigation. | Operator priority, measured scan/capture impact, availability of second SDR. | Multiple-SDR; optional IQ impact; scan revisit. | Investigation scheduling policy. |
| AQ-15 | What IQ-capture scope, duration, retention, access controls, and metadata are lawful and operationally appropriate? | IQ is high-volume evidence with governance and coverage implications. | Disable for MVP; enabled per profile/event; restricted operator request. | Legal/operational policy, storage impact, SigMF metadata validation, privacy review. | Optional IQ capture impact. | Enabling Evidence Services capture. |
| AQ-16 | Is there a justified classification use case with locally relevant lawful data, unknown handling, and calibrated uncertainty? | Prevents unsupported labels or coupling a research model to core monitoring. | Rule-based characterization only; offline research; bounded CM4 inference later. | Dataset provenance, test protocol, confusion/calibration results, CM4 cost, operator use case. | Signal classification feasibility. | Enabling classifier and label taxonomy. |
| AQ-17 | What report/heatmap resolution, historical span, and generation schedule are useful within CM4/storage limits? | Determines derived load and artifact retention without weakening source capture. | Daily batch; on-demand; precomputed aggregates; resolution tiers. | Operator needs and measured query/render/storage behavior. | Historical processing and report generation; disk experiment. | Analytics/artifact policy. |
